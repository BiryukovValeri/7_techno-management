## TV-N01 — 10 шагов: от ситуации к следующему решению
Purpose: canonical visualization of the CURRENT 10-step mechanism; replaces any temptation to use old 8-node viz-01.
Semantic content, exact order:
1 ситуация и цена ошибки
2 цели / интересы / критерии успеха
3 профиль участников
4 влияние / полномочия
5 BATNA
6 ZOPA
7 уступки / встречные шаги / роли
8 когнитивные риски
9 следующий сценарий решения
10 фиксация / разбор / улучшение
→ return arrow to next situation/cycle.
Visual form: architectural route/loop, not ten equal SaaS cards.
Primary placement: /technology/ T04.
Secondary: compact reduced route on Home H05 only if it does not duplicate text excessively.
Responsive: preserve order and return loop; mobile may become vertical route with explicit return-to-next-cycle connector.

## TV-N02 — Внутренний мандат → внешняя позиция
Purpose: show why external negotiating power is often determined before the external meeting.
Left/internal layer: owner/management + finance + sales/procurement + operations/project/legal where relevant → goal/minimum → authority → red lines → price of concessions → pause/exit owner.
Bridge: agreed mandate.
Right/external layer: participant/influence map → BATNA → ZOPA → packages/concessions → next decision.
Key statement embodied by diagram: no stable external position without internally agreed boundaries and authority.
Primary placement: /technology/ immediately after “one core / two fields”.
Optional reduced placement: Home H04.
Do not show an org chart. Do not imply fixed company structure.

## TV-N03 — Архитектура одного управленческого решения
Purpose: compress the technology into the decision logic a C-level reader can inspect at a glance.
Sequence:
Goal + minimum
→ BATNA: what if no agreement
→ boundary of bad agreement
→ ZOPA: where acceptable agreement may exist
→ package of terms
→ concession ↔ counterstep
→ authority/risk check
→ decision: continue / conditions / clarify / pause / exit
→ fixation for next cycle.
Primary placement: /technology/ after BATNA/ZOPA and before concessions/risk blocks, or /tools/ intro if page balance is better.
Do not duplicate TV-05 visually; TV-05 explains BATNA/ZOPA relationship, TV-N03 explains how that relationship becomes a management decision.

## Image Slots
Image Slots are contextual editorial images, not diagrams and not tool previews.

### IS-01 — Внутреннее согласование перед внешним раундом
Function: visually ground the idea that negotiation starts inside the company.
Scene: executive working table, documents/notes, several functional viewpoints visible through materials, boundaries/decision preparation; no staged handshake.
Placement: Home between H02–H04 OR Technology before TV-N02. Use once only.

### IS-02 — Переговоры как система участников
Function: break the false “two people at a table” stereotype.
Scene: professional negotiation environment showing multiple roles/decision centers, documents and attention paths; not confrontation, chess, duel or victory.
Placement: Technology around participant/influence section T09.
Must support the notion of roles/influence/authority, not depict personality profiling.

### IS-03 — После раунда: фиксация решения
Function: make Step 10 tangible.

Placement: Technology T12 or Tools before Form 23.
Do not use “celebration/success/deal signed” imagery.
