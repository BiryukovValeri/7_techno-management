## 2. What each layer must explain
### Home
Not an encyclopedia. It must assemble a coherent first understanding:
1. what negotiation position is being managed;
2. why external negotiation depends on internal alignment;
3. how the technology works as a cycle;
4. what decision outcomes are legitimate;
5. what instruments make the work material;
6. evidence of applicability without guarantees;
7. selected model cases;
8. exactly four ways to work;
9. route to deeper knowledge and contact.

Home may preview Articles/About but must not duplicate their full content.

### Technology
The canonical explanatory page. Required:
- object of management;
- one core / two fields;
- 10-step cycle;
- goals/interests/success criteria;
- participant profile, influence, authority;
- BATNA and ZOPA with Russian explanation;
- concessions/countersteps;
- cognitive decision risks;
- next-decision scenario;
- fixation/review/next cycle;
- valid outcomes;
- boundaries and anti-category.

### Tools
Forms 17–23 are shown as a connected working instrument system, not seven unrelated downloads/cards.
Required:
17 preparation/readiness;
18 goals + BATNA + ZOPA;
19 participant profile + influence;
20 scenarios + team roles;
21 concessions + countersteps;
22 cognitive risks;
23 post-negotiation report + indicators + next cycle.
No Form 24.

### Products
Exactly four modes:
Диагностика / Сессия / Спринт / Сопровождение.
The public architecture must not imply an automatic funnel. Transitions are conditional on situation/readiness/data.

### Cases
All 12 model/teaching cases remain discoverable.
Index may provide short summaries/filters grounded in source.
Each detail page must preserve a full public-safe body; summary cannot replace it.
Every case must visibly state model/teaching status and cannot be framed as testimonial/client result.

### Articles
A governed explanatory layer, not an SEO content farm.
Its job:
- answer management questions in search/answer contexts;
- deepen concepts that Home cannot carry;
- create meaningful internal links to Technology, Tools, Cases and Products;
- never invent evidence, outcomes or technology entities.

Permitted source-grounded topic lanes:
- переговорная позиция компании;
- подготовка к переговорам;
- BATNA / альтернатива соглашению;
- ZOPA / зона возможного соглашения;
- полномочия и роли;
- карта влияния;
- уступки и встречные шаги;
- когнитивные риски решения;
- внутреннее согласование позиции;
- разбор переговоров и следующий цикл;
- границы плохого соглашения;
- client/supplier/partner contexts only where corpus supports them.

Topic lane != published article. No empty/generated pages.

### About Project
Explains provenance and scope of the project using T1/M/C only:
- what Архитектура Переговоров is;
- what management object it works with;
- external/internal fields;
- public structure: cycle, tools, cases, four formats;
- boundaries.
No invented history/adoption/market achievements.

### About Author
Mandatory destination, but factual content is HOLD until verified owner/source material exists.
No invented biography, credentials, years, clients, employers, education, awards, media or quantified achievements.

### Contact
First-class conversion/trust destination.
Public copy can explain what information is useful to start from.
Actual email/phone/messenger/form endpoint and privacy/consent must be current and verified before publication.

- Home → Technology / Products / Cases / Tools / Articles / About / Contact
- Technology ↔ Tools; Technology → relevant Cases/Articles; Technology → Products

- Cases → Technology concepts and relevant Products only where the case text supports it
- Articles → canonical Technology/Tools/Cases pages by actual subject
- About → Technology / Author / Contact
- Author → About / Technology / Articles only after verified content exists
- All conversion-capable pages → Contact

No automated cross-linking by keyword alone.

A block exists only if it helps transfer required understanding.
A card/grid/image is not evidence of coverage.
Presence without comprehension = FAIL.
