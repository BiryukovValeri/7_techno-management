## Scope

## BATNA
**BATNA — Best Alternative to a Negotiated Agreement — лучшая альтернатива соглашению.**

Public meaning: the best realistically available course of action if an acceptable agreement is not reached. It is not an abstract «plan B»: alternatives must be identified, checked for feasibility/cost/time/risk/resources, compared, and the best available alternative selected.

Intellectual provenance: the concept/term is associated with Roger Fisher and William Ury and the Harvard negotiation tradition; current Harvard Program on Negotiation materials describe BATNA through *Getting to Yes* and later editions with Bruce Patton.

Why it is in our technology: BATNA supplies the reference point OUTSIDE the agreement. It helps distinguish a merely available deal from a deal that is better than the best realistic alternative, and supports a rational pause/exit decision.

Public micro-history:
- developed/popularized in the Harvard negotiation tradition;
- introduced to a broad management audience through *Getting to Yes*;
- remains a standard preparation concept in contemporary negotiation teaching and practice.

Trademark rule: do NOT claim BATNA is our trademark, our invention, or a registered mark of «Архитектура Переговоров». No trademark claim is needed on the public page.

## ZOPA
**ZOPA — Zone of Possible Agreement — зона возможного соглашения.**

Public meaning: the range/space of terms in which an agreement can potentially be acceptable to the parties. In simple bargaining this may be a numerical range; in business negotiation it can be multidimensional: price × volume × payment term × service × responsibility × exclusivity × execution × counter-obligations.

Intellectual provenance: ZOPA is a standard negotiation concept used in Harvard Program on Negotiation materials and negotiation literature. PON defines it as the bargaining range created by reservation values and cites Mnookin, Peppet and Tulumello, *Beyond Winning* (2004), in its glossary. Do NOT invent a single «author of ZOPA» unless a stronger primary historical source is established.

Why it is in our technology: ZOPA supplies the reference point INSIDE a potential agreement. BATNA answers «what do we do without agreement?»; ZOPA answers «where can an acceptable agreement exist?».
