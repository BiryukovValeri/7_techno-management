## Critical correction
The case corpus is a teaching/model application corpus, not a set of client testimonials or measured production outcomes. Public copy must not call these “real client cases”, “results”, or evidence of effectiveness. Model figures/roles are illustrative and must not be represented as actual company/network data.

## Public case index
A3-01 — Сад-Огород входит в условную сеть формата «Магнит». External. Decision: proceed only through a bounded pilot; protect economics/cash and exchange concessions for obligations.
A3-02 — Внутренний штаб перед входом в условную сеть формата «Магнит». Internal. Decision: no external round without an internal mandate, limits, pause right and decision owner.
A3-03 — Крупный клиент просит скидку и отсрочку. External. Decision: no isolated discount/deferral; use packages tied to volume, purchase schedule and return limits.
A3-04 — Поставщик дал скидку за объём, но забрал денежный контроль. External. Decision: evaluate discount against frozen cash/stock/dependency; use smaller or staged package.
A3-05 — Подрядчик сорвал срок и просил доплату. External. Decision: no payment for past failure without new measurable result; recovery package with milestones/responsibility or replacement.
A3-06 — Партнёр просил эксклюзивность без достаточного вклада. External. Decision: no full exclusivity without measurable contribution; pilot/partial exclusivity with minimum volume, contribution, reporting and revocation right.
A3-07 — Канал продаж обещал оборот и забирал контроль. External. Decision: pilot only with limits on discount/promo/data/supply and stop right; expand after useful revenue is confirmed.
A3-08 — Проект просил бюджет без владельца результата. Internal. Decision: no full budget without outcome owner; pilot, staged budget, checkpoint and stop right.
A3-09 — Продажи обещали срок, операции не могли исполнить. Internal. Decision: do not promise impossible full delivery; offer standard/accelerated/partial packages with explicit price/resources.
A3-10 — Команда не знала, кто имеет право сказать клиенту «нет». Internal. Decision: define authority ladder and refusal owner; account manager cannot unilaterally accept the client package.
A3-11 — Собственник хотел быстрый рост, команда видела риск плохих сделок. Internal. Decision: growth must pass useful-deal criteria including margin/cash/execution; low-margin deals require separate owner decision.
A3-12 — Юристы остановили сделку после коммерческого согласия. Internal. Decision: do not sign current draft; return legal risk to negotiation through liability limits, control-zone deadlines, staged acceptance and client obligations.

## Public disclosure rule
Each case page must visibly state that it is a model/teaching application example. Figures and roles are conditional unless a source explicitly says otherwise. The page may demonstrate how technology structures a decision; it may not claim the described result actually occurred, prove effectiveness, guarantee agreement, or substitute legal/financial advice.

## Case-detail template
1 Situation — model context and decision stake.
2 Management problem — what is being decided.
3 Technology objects — only objects actually present in the source case.
4 Work path — source-supported sequence.
5 Model decision — explicitly labelled as the decision produced inside the model case, not a client result.
6 What it demonstrates — bounded mechanism lesson.
7 Limitations — model figures/roles; no guarantee; no legal/financial conclusion.
8 Related product — only where the case source itself assigns diagnostics/session etc.
