For production continuity this document normalizes the current name as “Архитектура Переговоров”; the earlier wording remains a source alias, not a second product.

## 2. Definition
Архитектура Переговоров — технология управления переговорной силой компании во внешних и внутренних решениях.

“Переговорная сила” in the corpus is not domination over another party. It is the company’s ability to enter a negotiation/decision with clear goals, alternatives, boundaries, an influence map, controlled concessions and a defined next step.

## 3. Object of management
The technology manages the architecture of a concrete negotiation or internal decision situation:
- situation and price of error;
- interests and success criteria;
- participant/counterparty profile;
- influence, roles and authority;
- BATNA / best alternative;
- ZOPA / agreement zone;
- concession boundaries and countersteps;
- cognitive decision risks;
- scenario of the next decision;
- result fixation, review and next cycle.

It does not manage the other party’s will and does not guarantee agreement.

## 4. Target user / organizational context
Source-confirmed operating audience in current product protocols and commercial documentation:
- medium business;
- owner and management team.

Application participants vary by situation and may include sales, finance, procurement, operations/production, legal review, project teams and other decision participants. These roles are situational, not a fixed organization model.

## 5. Problem class
The technology is used when a company has a real external negotiation or internal agreement/decision where a poor decision can cost money, margin, time, control, relationships, obligations, resources or responsibility.

The source corpus repeatedly identifies failure modes the technology is designed to control:
- entering without a defined goal/minimum;
- dependence on one outcome because BATNA is weak or absent;
- inability to distinguish an acceptable agreement from a bad one;
- treating the formal negotiator as the whole decision system;
- concessions without a priced cost and counterstep;
- internal disagreement over authority, red lines or acceptable risk;
- decisions distorted by pressure, anchoring, loss aversion, groupthink or inertia;
- oral “results” without facts, owners, dates and next-cycle learning.

These are operating failure modes, not marketing promises of quantified loss.

## 6. One core, two application contours
### External contour
Clients, suppliers, partners, investors, contractors, retail chains and other external parties.
Sources of strength include value, terms, alternatives, refusal risk, counterparty map and controlled concessions.

### Internal contour
Owner, leadership, finance, sales, procurement, production/operations, project teams and other internal participants.
Sources of strength include authority, budget, responsibility, departmental interests, decision order, owner of decision and owner of pause.

The corpus explicitly treats these as two fields of application of one core, not two technologies.

## 7. Canonical mechanism
The current conduct-ready product protocols normalize the chain as:

**ситуация → цена ошибки → цель / минимум → профиль стороны → карта влияния → BATNA → ZOPA → уступки / встречные шаги → когнитивные риски → сценарий → фиксация → разбор → следующий цикл**

The official Technology 05 expresses the same mechanism as a 10-step repeatable cycle:
1. situation and price of error;
2. goals, interests and success criteria;
3. profile of counterparty/decision participant;
4. influence and power map;
5. BATNA;
6. ZOPA;
7. concession boundaries and team roles;
8. cognitive risks;
9. next-decision scenario;
10. fixation, review and PDCA/next cycle.

The difference in item count is representational: current protocols expand some outputs inside the same operating chain; no contradictory mechanism was found.

## 8. Inputs
Minimum source-confirmed inputs depend on the situation but include:
- a real negotiation/decision situation rather than an abstract training request;
- what is at stake / price of error;
- known parties and decision participants;
- current objectives, constraints and available data;
- authority/decision ownership where relevant;
- documents and facts supplied or authorized by the client;
- explicit list of missing data when evidence is incomplete.

The technology must not fabricate missing facts about counterparties or internal participants.

## 9. Operational instruments
V1 has exactly seven fixed working forms:
17. preparation checklist;
18. goals + BATNA + ZOPA map;
19. counterparty/decision-participant profile + influence map;
20. scenarios + team role map;
21. concession and counterstep matrix;
22. cognitive decision-risk map;
23. post-negotiation report + measures + improvement journal.

Technology 06 explicitly states that Form 24 is outside V1 and a new mandatory form requires a separate decision.

## 10. Outputs
Source-confirmed outputs include:
- situation/price-of-error map;
- goal, ideal, minimum and stop conditions;
- counterparty/participant working profile;
- influence/authority map;
- BATNA and actions to strengthen it;
- ZOPA and boundary of a bad agreement;
- concession boundaries, concession cost and countersteps;
- team roles and protection of red lines;
- cognitive-risk map and checks;
- next-decision scenario;
- documented result, disputed points, owners and deadlines;
- plan/fact, lessons and next-cycle changes.

## 11. Decision output
A correct output is not synonymous with “deal closed”.

The technology can legitimately lead to:
- continue;
- continue under conditions;
- clarify/collect data;
- align the internal position;
- change the package of terms;
- strengthen BATNA;
- take a pause;
- refuse/exit;
- define conditions for returning to negotiations.

This is a core technological property, not a sales disclaimer.

## 12. Product system
Technology truth fixes four core paid products:
1. **Диагностика** — identifies negotiation gaps and boundaries of a bad decision; current protocol ends with a decision gate.
2. **Сессия** — assembles a unified team position and negotiation design for a real situation.
3. **Спринт** — transfers the technology into repeated team practice across real negotiations/agreements.
4. **Сопровождение** — maintains the system over time through recurring situation selection, boundary/alternative checks, influence/concession/risk control and reporting.

Commercial B2 contains extended variants, Board-level wording and bundles. They are commercial derivatives/configurations, not additional core technologies/products for Technology Truth.

## 13. Product progression
The corpus supports a progression from entry diagnosis to design, implementation and sustained practice. However, a client’s exact route is situation-dependent; Technology Truth does not convert the commercial package table into a mandatory universal funnel.

## 14. Roles and decision rights
The technology requires explicit decision ownership and authority rather than a universal role chart.
Source-confirmed controls include:
- owner of the decision;
- who may authorize price/concession/risk/resource;
- who can call a pause;
- who protects red lines;
- who records the result;
- who owns actions and deadlines.

The technology does not create a fixed corporate organization model.

## 15. Evidence boundary
The read evidence summary covers four checks:
- external network/channel situation;
- internal cross-functional budget/resource decision;
- internal conflict over deal conditions/concessions;
- internal responsibility/authority situation.

The evidence document explicitly states that it is not proof of the whole technology. It supports bounded observations about form usability, internal/external coupling and identified overload risks. Therefore no universal percentage uplift, win rate, margin increase or causal business-effect number is part of Technology Truth.

## 16. Known implementation risks from tests
The corpus records risks including:
- turning the preparation checklist into a full audit;
- mixing BATNA and ZOPA;
- guessing motives in the participant profile;
- over-expanding team roles;
- insufficiently concrete concession-cost calculation;
- psychologizing cognitive risks.

The prescribed direction is to keep facts/assumptions/hypotheses separated, preserve BATNA/ZOPA distinction, include only decision-relevant roles, and tie risks to concrete decisions/checks.

## 17. Ethical and professional boundaries
The technology:
- does not guarantee victory;
- does not control the other party’s will;
- does not support hidden pressure, rumor gathering or bypassing responsible people;
- does not diagnose personalities;
- does not replace legal review;
- does not replace financial modeling/audit;
- does not replace security functions;
- does not make percentage/ROI claims without situation-specific evidence;
- uses only client-provided or client-authorized data;
- does not make the owner’s/business decision instead of the decision owner.

## 18. Anti-category
Архитектура Переговоров is **not**:
- a one-off negotiation training;
- a collection of pressure tricks;
- a script of “magic phrases”;
- a promise to win;
- psychological profiling of people;
- a financial audit;
- a legal opinion;
- a set of spreadsheets detached from a real management gap.

The distinction supported by the corpus is: skills/tricks focus on behavior in the conversation; this technology structures preparation, decision boundaries, influence, alternatives, concessions, risks, fixation and learning across the whole situation.

## 19. What must not be silently added later
Without new corpus evidence or explicit owner decision, later stages must not add:
- guaranteed win-rate, margin or ROI claims;
- psychological/personality scoring;
- covert influence/manipulation mechanics;
- a new mandatory Form 24+;
- new core products beyond the four fixed here;
- an AI layer/agents/copilots/automation as part of the technology;
- a fixed organization model;
- imported constructs from DA/UM, CLM, Assortment or other project technologies.
