## Pilot A — Technology Visualization
Object: TV-02 «Переговорное поле».
Source: Visualization 02.
Type: SOURCE VISUALIZATION → WEB ADAPTATION.
Target context: Technology page / explanation of one negotiation field with external and internal contours.

### Visitor question
«Почему переговорная позиция компании зависит не только от человека напротив меня, но и от внутреннего контура, полномочий, ограничений, неизвестного и влияния?»

### What the image must make clearer than text alone
The viewer must perceive one field containing external and internal actors plus the forces/conditions around the decision. It must visually distinguish:
- external side;
- internal contour;
- interests;
- constraints;
- decision authority;
- blind/unknown zone;
- pressure/risk point;
- confirmed vs unverified influence.

### Why a visualization is justified
These entities are relational. A prose list can name them but does not show that they coexist around one decision and that influence has different evidentiary status.

### Mandatory semantic invariants
Use TV_PRESERVATION_CONTRACTS.md / TV-02 verbatim as authority.
No new entities or claims.
No org chart.
No personality/motive inference.
No manipulation advice.

### Design freedom
Claude may choose web composition, geometry, density, typography, line system and responsive transformation inside the selected Design System. Claude does not have to imitate slide layout or Elementor widgets.

### Responsive invariant
On mobile the same field relationships must remain understandable. It is acceptable to reflow/recompose. It is not acceptable to shrink a desktop diagram until unreadable or to remove semantic nodes.

### Acceptance
Independent comparison against source and preservation contract. Claude self-report is not acceptance.
Any missing node/relation = SEMANTIC_DRIFT.
A newly invented diagram that changes source meaning = VISUAL_SUBSTITUTION.

## Pilot B — Image Slot
Object: IS-TOOLS — «Семь рабочих форм».
Type: IMAGE SLOT, not Technology Visualization.
Target context: HOME-07 and/or /tools/.

### Visitor question
«Это просто семь названий документов или реальный связанный рабочий комплект, который сопровождает ситуацию от подготовки до разбора?»

### Informational job
The image slot may make the materiality and connectedness of Forms 17–23 easier to grasp:
17 preparation;
18 goals/BATNA/ZOPA;
19 participant/influence;
20 scenarios/team roles;
21 concessions/countersteps;
22 cognitive risks;
23 post-negotiation report/indicators/improvement.

The visible HTML remains authoritative for names and explanations.

### Image necessity test
The slot survives only if the proposed image communicates BOTH:
1. these are working artifacts/forms rather than abstract concepts;
2. they belong to one connected work cycle rather than seven unrelated downloads/cards.
If the design adds only decoration, use NO IMAGE.

### Allowed visual content
- abstract/non-readable document/form materiality;
- connected dossier/workflow composition;
- numbering 17–23 only when reliably rendered or supplied as HTML overlay;
- visual continuity from preparation to post-negotiation review.

### Forbidden
- readable generated pseudo-text;
- invented fields, scores, metrics or form content;
- seven products;
- download-store treatment;
- SaaS dashboard;
- AI assistant/copilot;
- Hero dossier metaphor cloned wholesale;
- protected/internal XLSX detail exposed merely to make the image look technical.

### Responsive invariant
Mobile may show a compact sequence/stack, but must retain the sense of one connected working set. Do not require tiny unreadable form screenshots.

### Acceptance
The reviewer must answer:
- Does the slot materially improve comprehension? Y/N
- Does it communicate artifact materiality? Y/N
- Does it communicate one connected set? Y/N
- Does it introduce unsupported form detail? Y/N
- Does it look like a fifth/seventh product system? Y/N
Pass requires Y/Y/Y/N/N.
Otherwise: NO IMAGE or redesign.
