## TV-03 — BATNA / ZOPA
Source thesis: BATNA and ZOPA answer different questions of negotiation strength.

Mandatory nodes:
- BATNA / лучшая альтернатива.
- Усиление альтернативы.
- ZOPA / зона соглашения.
- Граница плохого соглашения.
- Сигнал паузы.
- Сигнал выхода.

Mandatory relations:
- BATNA is not ZOPA.
- Strengthening BATNA reduces dependence on one outcome.
- ZOPA is an acceptable range, not a guarantee.
- Bad-agreement boundary constrains acceptance.
- Pause and exit are legitimate decision signals.

Boundary: not a goals table, not calculation of the other side's desire, not a way to impose a range.

Public language: first meaningful public use explains BATNA as «лучшая альтернатива соглашению» and ZOPA as «зона возможного соглашения».

Forbidden: merge BATNA and ZOPA; imply guaranteed agreement; delete pause/exit; infer the other side's reservation value without data.
