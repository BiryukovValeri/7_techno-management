# HERO_CONCEPTS — 4 DISTINCT DIRECTIONS

Status: INTERNAL EXPLORATION — NOT AN OWNER PRODUCT/TECHNOLOGY CHOICE

## H1 — «Архитектурный разрез позиции»
**Core visual:** an executive architectural drawing / exploded plan of one negotiation position. Seven/eight structural layers align around a central “решение”: goal, authority, BATNA, boundary, influence, concessions, risks, next step.
**Meaning:** strength comes from assembled structure, not personal pressure.
**Composition:** copy left; large layered architectural object right; warm paper, navy technical lines, copper route, teal confirmed/controlled elements, red only at stop boundary.
**Distinctiveness:** directly literalizes the product name “Архитектура Переговоров”.
**Mobile:** central stacked layers remain readable; labels can reduce to 4 primary anchors.
**Risk:** can become too diagrammatic or resemble a slide if over-labelled.
**Control:** no full 10-step chart in Hero; use structure, not an infographic dump.

## H2 — «Поле решения»
**Core visual:** a spatial field with external side, internal company contour and a central decision boundary. Influence lines, authority nodes, alternatives and stop boundary show that the negotiation is wider than the meeting.
**Meaning:** the company's external position is formed by both external and internal decision geometry.
**Composition:** copy in a calm left/top zone; field map occupies remaining area; no portraits; nodes are roles/decision rights, not people profiles.
**Distinctiveness:** strongest expression of “external + internal” one-core/two-contour truth.
**Mobile:** preserve central boundary and two contours; secondary influence lines disappear.
**Risk:** could look like org/network analysis.
**Control:** label the object as negotiation/decision field; avoid organizational hierarchy.

## H3 — «Граница хорошего решения»
**Core visual:** a clean decision landscape with an explicit acceptable zone, bad-agreement boundary, BATNA route and several controlled next moves: continue / change / pause / exit.
**Meaning:** negotiation power includes the ability not to accept a bad agreement.
**Composition:** headline/copy left; decisive boundary geometry right; copper route approaches a red stop line but has teal alternative branches.
**Distinctiveness:** communicates the non-obvious decision-output logic immediately.
**Mobile:** boundary + alternative path survives extremely well.
**Risk:** too much emphasis on refusal/risk may shift the site back toward negative framing.
**Control:** frame as freedom of controlled choice, not danger avoidance.

## H4 — «Собранная позиция / Executive dossier»
**Core visual:** a premium physical/abstract dossier spread: several decision cards/sheets (goal, authority, alternative, concessions, next step) mechanically align into one compact “Position / Decision” master sheet.
**Meaning:** scattered knowledge becomes one explicit company position.
**Composition:** strong editorial headline left; tactile executive dossier on right, angled minimally; no fake app UI.
**Distinctiveness:** less like consulting diagram, more like a proprietary working technology.
**Mobile:** master sheet becomes dominant; supporting cards crop naturally.
**Risk:** may be mistaken for a document/template product.
**Control:** show relationships and decision movement, not merely stationery.

## Shared exclusions
No people shaking hands; no chess pieces; no boxing/duel; no boardroom stock photo; no glowing AI HUD; no “win” trophy; no aggressive red; no fake metrics.
