## Decision
Use one primary Hero direction: **Архитектура позиции**.

This is a site-first solution, not a new technology construct. It directly visualizes the locked governing message: a company's negotiation position is assembled before and between meetings from controlled decision objects.

## First-screen copy

Eyebrow:
**АРХИТЕКТУРА ПЕРЕГОВОРОВ**

H1:
**Переговорная позиция компании создаётся до того, как принято решение**

Subhead:
Цели, полномочия, альтернативы, границы уступок, влияние, риски и следующий шаг собираются в одну управляемую архитектуру — для внешних и внутренних переговоров.

Primary CTA:
**Разобрать переговорную ситуацию**

Secondary CTA:
**Как работает технология**

Support line:
Диагностика · Сессия · Спринт · Сопровождение

## Visual composition
Desktop:
- left 46–50%: eyebrow, H1, subhead, two CTAs, four-product support line;
- right 50–54%: one architectural object, not a dashboard and not a full infographic;
- object consists of a central decision plane plus 7 coordinated layers/nodes: цель, полномочия, альтернатива, границы, влияние, уступки, риски/следующий шаг;
- lines show assembly into one position, not a sequential product funnel;
- warm light field; deep navy structural lines; copper route/connection; muted teal controlled/confirmed elements; diagnostic red only for boundary/stop;
- no people, handshake, chess, duel, trophy, fake metrics, AI interface.

Mobile:
- copy first;
- primary CTA;
- simplified architecture object with 4 visible anchors: цель / полномочия / альтернатива / граница;
- secondary CTA;
- four-product support line.

## Why this direction
- literal fit with the public technology name;
- expresses company-level management rather than personal negotiation skill;
- supports both external and internal contours without explaining the whole site in Hero;
- does not alter the four-product ontology;
- creates a visual grammar that can continue into the site architecture and semantic diagrams.

## Product lock
The technology has exactly four core products:
1. Диагностика
2. Сессия
3. Спринт
4. Сопровождение

Hero does not create a fifth product and does not redefine these four.
