Status: CANDIDATE DIRECTIONS FOR OWNER SELECTION
Date: 2026-09-28
Input: Gate M LOCK

## Communication job
In the first screen the visitor must understand:
1. this is a management technology for a company, not a negotiation course;
2. it works with a consequential external or internal negotiation/decision;
3. its value is to assemble an explicit, controlled position before the decision;
4. there is a concrete way to enter the technology without a promise of victory.

## Required meaning
The visual should make **architecture of position** visible: several decision objects are coordinated into one controllable whole.

## Must not imply
- duel, war, chess, domination or winner/loser;
- psychological reading/manipulation;
- AI/software dashboard;
- generic sales training;
- guaranteed agreement/result;
- mandatory four-product ladder;
- Diagnostics as mandatory entry for every visitor.
