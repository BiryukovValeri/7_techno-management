# GOVERNING_SITE_CONCEPT — Архитектура Переговоров

Status: FINAL FOR OWNER GATE M
Date: 2026-09-28

## Category
Technology for managing a company's negotiation power in consequential external and internal decisions.

## Core value
Make the company's position explicit and manageable before the decision: goal, minimum, alternative, authority, influence, concession boundaries, risks and next step.

## Governing message
**Сильная переговорная позиция компании создаётся до и между встречами — когда цели, полномочия, альтернативы, границы уступок, влияние, риски и следующий шаг собраны в одну управляемую архитектуру.**

## Site idea
The site should make visible something that is usually scattered across people, meetings and documents: **the architecture of the company's position in a real negotiation/decision**.

The visitor should not be taken through a fear funnel. The cognitive movement is:
**“переговоры как разговор” → “переговоры как управляемая система решения” → “я вижу её объекты и цикл” → “я вижу, какой формат работы нужен для моей ситуации”.**

## Hero text logic
Name the category/object → state the management value → give a concrete entry action.
Do not lead with BATNA/ZOPA jargon, pain, tricks, victory or training language.

## Proof hierarchy
Mechanism → Forms 17–23 → four operating products → bounded checks → approved cases later.
No unread case, visual or commercial number can jump above this hierarchy.

## Conversion principle
Conversion follows the required depth of work:
Diagnostics / Session / Sprint / Support.
No artificial mandatory ladder is asserted unless the product evidence requires it for a specific situation.

## Anti-category
Not training, manipulation, scripts, psychological profiling, spreadsheets-as-product, guaranteed win, legal/financial opinion or AI negotiation software.

## Constraint for S04+
Visual and structural concepts must express architecture, coordination, boundaries, alternatives, influence and decision movement without war/chess/duel clichés or fake analytical UI.
