## 1. Category
**Технология управления переговорной силой компании в конкретных внешних и внутренних решениях.**

The category is deliberately not “обучение переговорам”, “тренинг переговорщиков”, “набор техник влияния” or “консалтинг по продажам”. The source corpus manages the architecture of a real decision: objectives, alternatives, authority, influence, concessions, risks, scenario, fixation and review.

## 2. Primary audience
Primary decision-maker: owner / top management of a medium business facing material external or internal negotiations/decisions.

Situation-level participants may include sales, finance, procurement, operations/production, legal/project roles and other functions. They are participants in the decision system, not separate buyer personas invented for the site.

## 3. Value
**Before entering a consequential negotiation or decision, make the company's position explicit, bounded and manageable — so the team knows what it wants, what it can concede, what alternative it has, who can decide, where the bad-agreement boundary is and what happens next.**

Value is decision quality/control, not a guaranteed agreement or “victory”.

## 4. Governing message
**Сильная переговорная позиция компании создаётся до и между встречами — когда цели, полномочия, альтернативы, границы уступок, влияние, риски и следующий шаг собраны в одну управляемую архитектуру.**

This message unifies external and internal contours without reducing the technology to personal negotiating skill.

## 5. Supporting messages
1. **Переговоры — это не только разговор за столом.** The result is affected by preparation, internal alignment, authority, alternatives, concessions and follow-through.
2. **Внешняя позиция начинается внутри компании.** If functions disagree on goal, red lines, authority or concession cost, the external position is weakened before the meeting.
3. **Не всякое соглашение — хороший результат.** Continue under conditions, clarify data, pause, change package, strengthen BATNA or exit can be valid decisions.
4. **Переговорная сила должна быть разложена на управляемые объекты.** The corpus supplies a 10-step cycle and Forms 17–23.
5. **Технология работает с реальной ситуацией, а не с абстрактным навыком.** Diagnostics, Session, Sprint and Support are tied to concrete decisions and artifacts.
6. **Факт, предположение и гипотеза не должны смешиваться.** Especially in participant profiling, influence and cognitive-risk work.
7. **Результат должен оставлять след.** Decision, disputed points, owners, deadlines and next-cycle learning are fixed.

## 6. Differentiation
The source-supported differentiation is structural, not competitive boasting:
- manages a company decision architecture rather than only an individual's communication skill;
- joins external negotiation and internal alignment through one core;
- explicitly links BATNA, ZOPA, authority/influence, concessions/countersteps, cognitive risks and scenario;
- includes post-negotiation fixation and learning, not only preparation/conduct;
- permits refusal/pause/change as valid controlled outcomes;
- has a fixed V1 instrument architecture: Forms 17–23;
- separates decision-relevant participant modelling from psychological diagnosis.

No claim of uniqueness, “best method”, superior win rate or market leadership is authorized.

## 7. Anti-category
Do not position the technology as:
- training in persuasion;
- “secret techniques” or magic phrases;
- manipulation / reading people;
- personality typing;
- a promise to win every negotiation;
- sales scripts;
- legal or financial expertise;
- a collection of spreadsheets;
- AI negotiation automation;
- generic strategy/management consulting detached from a concrete negotiation/decision.

## 8. Evidence hierarchy
Public proof must follow this order:
1. **Mechanism proof:** canonical 10-step cycle and explicit managed objects.
2. **Instrument proof:** Forms 17–23 at approved name/role level.
3. **Product proof:** four current operating protocols and their concrete outputs/decision gates.
4. **Bounded evidence:** four checks from Technology 10 with their limitations stated.
5. **Cases:** only after case bodies are READ and disclosure/provenance is approved.
6. **Commercial data:** only as current offer information, never as proof of effectiveness.
7. **Primary book:** no direct proof claim while TECHNICAL HOLD remains.

A visual asset is never proof by itself.

## 9. Conversion logic
The conversion path should move by decision readiness, not fear escalation:

**recognize the management object → understand the architecture → see how it operates on a real situation → identify the appropriate depth of intervention → choose the first justified product/action.**

Product logic:
- Diagnostics when the first need is to expose gaps/boundaries and obtain a decision gate;
- Session when a real situation needs a unified team position/design;
- Sprint when the technology must be applied repeatedly across rounds and implemented;
- Support when the system must be maintained across a stream of situations.

Default first-step conversion may point to Diagnostics only where the visitor's situation matches its role. The site must not falsely imply that every visitor is required to buy all four products sequentially.

## 10. Terminology
Preferred public name: **Архитектура Переговоров**.
Use Russian first; explain necessary English terms:
- BATNA — лучшая альтернатива соглашению;
- ZOPA — зона возможного соглашения.

Preferred language:
- переговорная позиция;
- переговорная сила;
- границы плохого соглашения;
- полномочия;
- карта влияния;
- уступка / цена уступки / встречный шаг;
- сценарий следующего решения;
- когнитивный риск решения;
- фиксация результата;
- следующий цикл.

Avoid inflated abstractions such as “доминирование”, “взлом контрагента”, “психотип”, “непобедимая позиция”, “гарантированный результат”.

## 11. Prohibited claims
No:
- guaranteed agreement/victory;
- universal ROI/margin/win-rate effect;
- causal business KPI claims not evidenced;
- psychological diagnosis or motive-as-fact;
- covert manipulation;
- claims that unread cases prove results;
- claim that the primary book was read in this run;
- new forms/products/AI capability;
- imported constructs from other 7vctr technologies.

## 12. Hero communication implications
Hero must communicate in one screen:
1. this is a company management technology, not a negotiation course;
2. the object is a consequential external or internal negotiation/decision;
3. the value is a controlled position before the decision: goals, alternatives, boundaries, authority, concessions, risks and next step;
4. there is a concrete next action.

Hero must NOT:
- start from “you negotiate badly” or humiliation/fear;
- promise victory;
- show adversarial domination, chess-war clichés or psychological manipulation;
- turn BATNA/ZOPA acronyms into the main headline;
- imply software/AI product;
- overload the first screen with the whole 10-step method.

Exact Hero copy is S04, not locked by M.

## 13. Message discipline
The site should lead from constructive managerial control, not from a catalogue of failures. Problems may explain stakes, but the governing frame is **building an explicit, controllable negotiation position**.

Every downstream message must trace either to T1 truth or S02 public-safe evidence. Narrow facts may not be inflated into whole-technology promises.
