## 1. Positioning problem
The first S03 version over-positioned the technology as a route from problem to execution. That mechanism is true but category-generic.

The deeper repeated corpus proposition is different:
- a management situation can support many plausible strategic explanations and actions;
- functions hold conflicting versions of reality;
- a large framework/tool library increases choice rather than solving it;
- the cost of choosing the wrong route is time, money, margin, team capacity and trust;
- therefore the primary job is to reduce the decision space using evidence before committing the company to action.

SOURCE CONFIRMED anchors:
- Content Architecture v1.0 canonical brand formula: not choose from hundreds of strategic models; receive a narrow set under an evidenced management task and connect it to execution.
- Client Semantic Site Model V1: client job includes choosing the strategic route and reducing risk of the wrong step; visual dramaturgy is narrowing chaos to a working route.
- STRAT Table v0.2: 100-model index is internal; the client needs transparent logic for why only 5–7 enter the Session.
- Operating System/Evidence Matrix: symptom is not diagnosis; selection is gated by Problem Unit, triggers and evidence; Hold/Stop are valid.
- Case Navigator: cases prohibit premature winner selection and simplistic causal conclusions.

## 2. Category
Public category:
**экспертная система управленческого выбора.**

Expanded category:
**проSTRAнство — экспертная система, которая сужает множество возможных стратегических решений до обоснованного рабочего выбора для конкретной управленческой ситуации и проверяет его через операционное исполнение.**

“Управленческая технология, связывающая стратегию и операции” remains a descriptive mechanism, not the positioning.

## 3. Primary audience
Owner / CEO / first management circle facing a consequential decision where:
- several plausible routes compete;
- functions disagree about causes/priorities;
- the problem crosses strategy, operations, finance, customer and organization;
- acting on the wrong interpretation is expensive;
- another generic strategy session or framework list will not resolve the choice.

## 4. Core value
**Снизить цену неверного управленческого выбора.**

проSTRAнство does this not by predicting a guaranteed outcome, but by:
1. converting symptom into evidenced Problem Unit;
2. distinguishing what is broken from what already works and must not be touched;
3. narrowing triggers and candidate strategic approaches;
4. rejecting irrelevant/specialized choices;
5. translating the selected strategic output through Bridge into a limited operational set;
6. testing the choice with owners, baseline, metrics and rhythm;
7. allowing Conditional Go / Hold / Stop instead of forcing continuation.

This value is stronger than “route to execution”: execution is the verification layer of the choice.

## 5. Governing message
**Не больше управленческих решений. Точнее выбор.**

Expanded governing proposition:
**Когда вариантов много, задача руководителя — не найти ещё одну модель, а доказательно сузить пространство выбора до решения, которое имеет основания именно для этой ситуации. проSTRAнство делает этот выбор проверяемым — от диагноза до испытания в операционном контуре.**

This is a positioning proposition, not final Hero copy. Exact Hero wording belongs to S04.

## 6. Strategic idea of the name
INTERPRETATION grounded in corpus:
“проSTRAнство” can be semantically used as the **пространство управленческого выбора**:
- at entry it is broad, noisy and contradictory;
- Diagnostics establishes reality and boundaries;
- Session narrows alternatives;
- Bridge makes the chosen route executable;
- Sprint tests whether the narrowed choice survives reality;
- Support checks whether it remains stable.

This interpretation is consistent with the corpus phrase “сужение хаоса до рабочего маршрута” and the internal selection architecture. It must not be presented as historical etymology unless owner confirms it as author intent.

## 7. Differentiation
The technology’s differentiation is **selection discipline**, not library size.

Not:
100 frameworks → choose one.
But:
problem evidence → triggers → bounded candidate field → justified narrow working set → Bridge → limited operational set → real-world verification.

The 115-core and 95 accepted STR support the proposition “selection from depth”, but never become the offer itself.

Distinctive behaviors:
- right to conclude “do not touch”;
- right to Hold/Stop;
- explicit rejection of premature tool selection;
- 1–3 linked triggers per Session;
- 5–7 strategic models as working set, not parade;
- 3–5 operational instruments in Sprint;
- baseline required before result claims.

## 8. Anti-category
проSTRAнство is not:
- strategic session;
- audit/report;
- framework catalog/library;
- outsourced management/project office;
- training;
- AI product;
- universal transformation program.

Most important false category:
**“another methodology that tells management what to do.”**
Correct frame:
**a system that establishes what is justified to do — and what is not — in the specific situation.**

## 9. Proof hierarchy
1. **Selection logic** — show how broad ambiguity is narrowed.
2. **Evidence discipline** — Problem Unit, facts, triggers, four verdicts, “do not touch”.
3. **Execution test** — Bridge, owners, baseline, metrics, rhythm, Sprint.
4. **Cases** — demonstrate different management situations producing different routes, including Hold/Stop.
5. **Expert depth** — 95 STR / 115 core as bounded evidence that narrow selection is not caused by a shallow library.
6. **Products/commercial facts** — explain how the client enters and proceeds.

## 10. Conversion logic
The site first sells the value of **better selection before commitment**, not Diagnostics as a category.

Cognitive conversion:
“I need another solution”
→ “my real risk is choosing a plausible but wrong solution”
→ “the choice must be narrowed by evidence”
→ “I understand how проSTRAнство selects and rejects”
→ “I can buy the first bounded step without buying a transformation”
→ qualification for paid Diagnostics.

Commercial entry remains:
qualification → paid Diagnostics.

## 11. Supporting messages
- The number of available frameworks is not an advantage if the company cannot establish which one applies now.
- A plausible management hypothesis is not yet a basis for action.
- What already works is part of the diagnosis; change is not a default.
- Strategy becomes valuable only after the selected route survives translation into owners, metrics and operating rhythm.
- Hold and Stop are products of precision, not failures of consulting.
- The technology narrows action before it expands execution.

## 12. Terminology
Public name: проSTRAнство.
Primary language: Russian.
First-layer vocabulary: выбор, основания, управленческая ситуация, проблема, факты, решение, исполнение, проверка.
Second-layer technical vocabulary: проблемная единица / Problem Unit; мост / Bridge; Go / Hold / Stop.
Avoid first-screen engineering jargon and internal codes.

## 13. Prohibited claims / implications
No guaranteed result; no “correct decision guaranteed”; no prediction certainty; no automatic Session/Sprint; no tool access promise; no named-client proof from generalized cases; no “115 solutions”; no AI magic; no replacement of management.

The safe promise is **higher discipline and evidentiary quality of selection**, not guaranteed business outcome.

## 14. Hero communication implications — semantic only
Hero must communicate:
- a field of plausible choices exists;
- the value is disciplined narrowing, not adding options;
- the output is not an abstract recommendation: the selected route is tested in execution.

Hero must not be built yet and must not default to:

- generic transformation imagery;
- dashboard/SaaS metaphor;
- library/catalog;
- diagnostics-only framing.
