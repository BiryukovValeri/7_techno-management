# MESSAGE_STRATEGY — проSTRAнство
Stage: S03
Status: FINAL FOR OWNER GATE M
Dependencies: T1 LOCK; S02 PASS

## 1. Category
SOURCE CONFIRMED + INTERPRETATION:
Public category: **управленческая технология, связывающая стратегический выбор с операционным исполнением**.
The operating corpus also defines проSTRAнство as an expert system. For first-contact communication, “экспертная система” is a supporting explanation, not the sole category label: the client must first understand the management job, not the internal form.

Category must not collapse into:
- strategic session;
- audit;
- consulting tool catalog;
- project office;
- training;
- AI product.

## 2. Primary audience
Primary:
- owner;
- CEO / general director;
- first management circle where mandate exists.

Situation:
there is a consequential management task, but strategy, operations, money/client signals, owners, metrics and execution do not form one verifiable contour.

The communication does NOT start from “your management is bad”. It starts from the need to connect decision and execution where the task crosses functions and competing versions of reality.

## 3. Core value
**Make the route from management problem to executable, verifiable action explicit before the company commits to unnecessary work.**

Value components:
1. distinguish symptom/opinion from evidenced Problem Unit;
2. preserve what already works;
3. narrow strategic choice instead of expanding a model menu;
4. connect choice to operations through Bridge;
5. attach owners, metrics and rhythm;
6. test the contour in Sprint 30-60-90;
7. permit Hold/Stop when continuation is not justified.

## 4. Governing message
**Стратегический выбор должен доходить до операционного исполнения — через проверенную проблему, владельцев, метрики и управленческий ритм. проSTRAнство собирает и проверяет этот маршрут.**

This is the governing semantic proposition for the site.
It does not promise a result before evidence and does not reduce the technology to Diagnostics alone.

## 5. Supporting messages
M1. Before choosing a solution, establish what is actually broken and what must not be touched.
M2. Strategy is not finished when a framework is selected; it must be translated into an operational carrier.
M3. A management decision is incomplete without owner, metric, rhythm and evidence.
M4. The technology deliberately narrows the working set; 115 is expert coverage, not a menu.
M5. A valid outcome may be Go, Conditional Go, Hold or Stop.
M6. The four products are conditional stages of one route, not a compulsory bundle.
M7. The company’s management remains the owner of execution; the technology does not replace it.

## 6. Differentiation
The differentiator is not “more frameworks” and not “a better strategy workshop”.
It is the controlled connection:
**Problem Unit → evidence → triggers → strategic working set → Bridge → operational instruments → owners/metrics/rhythm → Sprint verification.**

Public differentiation may use:
- evidence-gated transitions;
- explicit right to Hold/Stop;
- preservation of “do not touch” elements;
- Bridge between strategic choice and operations;
- limited working set selected from a larger internal expert core;
- baseline and decision log before claiming Sprint result.

Do not differentiate by unsupported superiority claims (“best”, “unique on the market”, “guaranteed”).

## 7. Anti-category
The site must actively prevent five false readings:
1. “This is a strategic session.”
2. “This is an audit/report.”
3. “This is a catalog of 115 tools.”
4. “This is outsourced management/project office.”
5. “This is training or AI transformation.”

The test from Language Standard applies: if a page can reasonably be read as one of these, messaging is not ready.

## 8. Proof hierarchy
P1 — mechanism proof:
show the actual route from Problem Unit to verified execution.

P2 — evidence discipline:
facts/data/artifacts/owners/baseline/transition gates; four verdicts.

P3 — product artifacts:
what each stage produces and what allows/refuses transition.

P4 — generalized cases:
12 problem patterns as mirrors of management situations, never named-client outcome claims.

P5 — expert depth:
115 accepted internal instrument core and 95 accepted strategic frameworks, only as bounded proof of selection depth; never the primary value proposition or menu.

P6 — commercial facts:
current Diagnostics format/price/duration where commercially useful, clearly separated from method truth.

## 9. Conversion logic
The site does not try to sell the full four-stage route in one action.

Primary conversion:
**qualify for paid Diagnostics.**

User logic:
recognize a consequential cross-functional management task
→ understand why jumping directly to solutions/Session is unsafe
→ understand the проSTRAнство mechanism
→ see evidence discipline and boundaries
→ see relevant generalized examples
→ understand Diagnostics as the controlled first paid step
→ qualification.

Important distinction:
The **site’s governing value is the whole route problem→execution**.
The **commercial entry point is Diagnostics**.
Do not turn the whole site into “buy an audit”.

## 11. Prohibited claims / implications
- ready strategy after first conversation;
- free implementation plan;
- guaranteed financial/business outcome;
- automatic Session after Diagnostics;
- automatic Sprint;
- “access to all 115 tools”;
- 115 as an operational set to deploy;
- named-client proof from generalized cases;
- AI as standalone technology function;
- consultant replaces management;
- “we diagnose people / find guilty functions”.

## 12. Hero communication implications (NOT Hero design)
Hero must make two things legible immediately:
1. this is about connecting strategic choice with operational execution;
2. the connection is controlled/verified, not motivational or workshop-based.

Hero must not imply:
- “Diagnostics is the whole technology”;
- generic consulting transformation;
- software dashboard/SaaS unless explicitly explained;
- catalog/library;
- magic AI;
- guaranteed growth.

Exact H1/subtitle/CTA and visual concept belong to S04 after Gate M.
