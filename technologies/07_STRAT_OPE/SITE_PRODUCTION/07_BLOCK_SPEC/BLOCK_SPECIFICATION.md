# S07 — BLOCK SPECIFICATION — проSTRAнство
Status: FINAL
Dependencies: T1 / M / H / A LOCK; S06 complete
Purpose: define what every block must communicate and contain before S08 writes source-locked final copy.

## Global block contract
Every block must have:
1. one semantic job;
2. one dominant question/proposition;
3. source-safe content boundary;
4. explicit evidence/artifact where relevant;
5. CTA only when it advances the visitor decision;
6. desktop/tablet/mobile semantic order;
7. no invented outcome, metric, client, testimonial or mechanism.

Internal engineering terms may be explained but not dumped into first-layer copy. English terms retained only where they are canonical technology names and should be paired with Russian meaning in public copy.

# HOMEPAGE

## HOME-01 — HERO
Job: category positioning + first conversion.
Eyebrow: expert system of management choice.
H1 LOCK: **Не больше управленческих решений. Точнее выбор.**
Required support meaning: when several solutions look reasonable, establish which has grounds in this situation and test it in action.
Primary CTA: **Разобрать управленческую ситуацию**
Secondary CTA: **Как работает технология**
Evidence burden: no quantitative claim.
Visual: owner-locked canonical Hero image only.
Do not add feature badges, counters, dashboard overlays or 95/115 here.
Mobile order: eyebrow → H1 → support → CTA → image.

## HOME-02 — WHY A REASONABLE SOLUTION IS NOT ENOUGH
Job: reframe the management task from idea-generation to justified selection.
Dominant question: **Почему разумного решения недостаточно?**
Required propositions:
- one symptom can support several plausible explanations;
- different functions can see different valid fragments;
- familiar strategic models can all look applicable;
- intervention can damage a working part of the contour.
Closing line candidate: **Правдоподобность — ещё не основание для действия.**
Evidence type: technology logic; no market-statistics needed.
CTA: none.
Do not dramatize “chaos” or incompetence.

## HOME-03 — HOW THE SPACE OF CHOICE NARROWS
Job: expose core mechanism.
Dominant question: **Как сужается пространство выбора?**
Required ordered stages:
1 complaint/symptom;
2 Problem Unit — verified management problem;
3 current contour — works / broken / do-not-touch;
4 confirmed triggers;
5 bounded strategic working set;
6 Bridge — translation from strategic output to operational carrier;
7 bounded operational set;
8 Sprint test.
Required conclusion: system reduces what has the right to move into action.
Evidence/artifact anchors: Problem Unit map, trigger register, Bridge, Sprint charter.
CTA: link to /how-it-works/.
Do not show 115-tool library.

## HOME-04 — GROUNDS / EVIDENCE
Job: explain why a candidate remains admissible.
Dominant question: **На каком основании вариант остаётся в работе?**
Required evidence chain:
checked conclusion → fact → source → artifact → verification owner → impact/verdict → stop-line.
Required diagnostic verdicts:
- Go;
- Conditional Go;
- Hold;
- Stop.
Explain in Russian; canonical English status may remain secondary.
Required proposition: existence of report/process/metric is not proof that it works.
CTA: optional link to Diagnostics.
Do not convert verdicts into red/yellow/green traffic-light simplification.

## HOME-05 — VALUE OF REJECTION
Job: differentiate through what the system prevents.
Dominant question: **Что проSTRAнство помогает не сделать?**
Required examples:
- not treat symptom as cause;
- not rebuild what is already working;
- not select strategic framework without confirmed trigger;
- not move solution to Sprint without Bridge;
- not launch more than bounded operational set;
- not continue without facts/mandate/owner/object.
Closing meaning: precision is visible in justified non-action as well as action.
CTA: none.
Tone: disciplined, not fear-based.

## HOME-06 — CHOICE MEETS REALITY
Job: establish that execution is a test of choice quality.
Dominant question: **Как выбранное решение встречается с реальностью?**
Required chain:
strategic output → operational carrier → owner → metric → rhythm → baseline → 30/60/90 observation → verdict.
Required artifacts: baseline, decision log, Sprint verdict.
Required proposition: execution is not merely the end of strategy; it tests whether the choice holds.
CTA: /sprint/.
No guaranteed implementation result.

## HOME-07 — PRODUCT CONTOUR
Job: explain commercial/service route without false funnel.
Dominant question: **Как устроена работа с компанией?**
Four cards:
### Diagnostics
Purpose: verify problem, contour, evidence and readiness.
Boundary: mandatory paid entry.
Possible result: including Hold/Stop; Session is not automatic.
### Session
Prerequisite: supported transition after Diagnostics.
Purpose: narrow strategic/operational choice; build Bridge and Sprint charter.
### Sprint 30–60–90
Prerequisite: Bridge and charter.
Purpose: test 3–5 operational instruments in action with owners/baseline/metrics/rhythm.
### Support
Prerequisite: active post-Sprint object.
Purpose: test sustainability/drift and decide continue/reduce/new Sprint/rebuild/exit.
Required visual note: return/stop paths exist.
CTA per card: “Подробнее”.
Primary section CTA: **Разобрать управленческую ситуацию**.
Do not imply bundle purchase.

## HOME-08 — WHAT THE CLIENT RECEIVES
Job: make technology tangible through artifacts.
Dominant question: **Что остаётся у руководителя после работы?**
Group 1 Diagnostics:
Problem Unit map; current-contour map; trigger register; diagnostic verdict.
Group 2 Session:
STR working set; OPE working set; Bridge map; Sprint charter.
Group 3 Sprint:
baseline; decision log; Sprint verdict.
Group 4 Support:
active-contour passport; drift map; Support verdict.
Required explanation: artifact counts as ready only when readiness criteria are met; elapsed time does not complete a stage.
CTA: none or mechanism link.
Do not invent downloadable samples before approved assets exist.

## HOME-09 — EXPERT DEPTH WITHOUT CATALOG
Job: prove selection depth while protecting positioning.
Dominant question: **Откуда берётся выбор, если на сайте нет каталога?**
Source-safe facts:
- STR source catalog: 100;
- accepted unique STR after duplicate/exclusion: 95;
- OPE source catalog: 100;
- FIN source instruments: 6;
- client-intelligence source instruments: 10;
- accepted unique instrument core: 115.
Public emphasis:
95 unique strategic frameworks / 115 accepted instrument core only if explanatory context is adjacent.
Required boundaries:
- not a menu;
- not a promise of “115 solutions”;
- Session works with bounded set;
- 5–7 strategic frameworks may form working set;
- ultimately 3–5 operational instruments launched in Sprint.
Core line candidate: **Глубина библиотеки нужна системе, чтобы клиенту не пришлось выбирать из библиотеки.**
CTA: /technology/.
No searchable/filterable tool UI.

## HOME-10 — CASES
Job: demonstrate route variance and evidence-driven change of choice.
Dominant question: **Как меняется выбор в разных управленческих ситуациях?**
Homepage: 3 representative generalized cases, selected in S08 from actually read/approved case corpus.
Card fields:
1 situation;
2 initially plausible direction;
3 evidence that changed/narrowed view;
4 selected/rejected direction;
5 verdict/next step.
Required label: generalized management example; not named-company outcome.
CTA: **Смотреть кейсы**.
No invented percentages, logos or testimonials.
H-002 applies until individual case sources are read for any detailed publication.

## HOME-11 — ANTI-CATEGORY
Job: prevent category misclassification.
Heading: **Что это — и чем проSTRAнство не является**
Pairs:
- not ordinary strategy session → expert selection system with evidence and transition rules;
- not audit/report → artifacts lead to decision/transition/verdict;
- not framework catalog → system narrows working set;
- not outsourced PMO → client owners remain owners;
- not training → work occurs on a concrete management object;
- not AI product → AI is not a separate technology function.
CTA: /technology/.
No competitor comparisons.

## HOME-12 — ENTRY / QUALIFICATION
Job: qualify before commercial action.
Heading LOCK candidate: **Начать не с решения. Начать с ситуации.**
Required qualification:
- management problem/pain exists;
- sponsor/mandate exists;
- access to facts/data is possible;
- readiness to accept adverse verdict.
Explain: first paid stage is Diagnostics; request does not commit client to full contour.
Primary CTA: **Разобрать управленческую ситуацию**
No “free audit”, “instant score” or guaranteed recommendation.

Job: remove category/process/purchase objections.

2 Can Diagnostics end with Hold or Stop?
3 Is Session automatic after Diagnostics?
4 Why only 3–5 instruments in Sprint?
5 What if part of current contour already works?
6 Is this consulting or project office?
7 What evidence/data is required?
8 What happens after 90 days?
Answers in S08 must be sourced from current canonical corpus/FAQ.
CTA: none inside each answer.

## HOME-14 — FINAL CTA
Job: close on positioning, not urgency.
Headline candidate: **Не больше решений. Больше оснований для выбора.**
Support: start with qualification for paid Diagnostics; no obligation to continue through all products.
CTA: **Разобрать управленческую ситуацию**
