## 1. Governing UX principle
The site must not begin with a generic “problem → solution → benefits” funnel.
Its page story must make the visitor experience the positioning:
**many plausible choices → grounds for narrowing → justified choice → translation into execution → verification by reality.**

Commercial conversion remains qualification → paid Diagnostics, but Diagnostics must not replace the technology definition.

## 3. Homepage architecture

### HOME-01 — HERO
Role: position the category and establish the central tension.
Eyebrow: **ЭКСПЕРТНАЯ СИСТЕМА УПРАВЛЕНЧЕСКОГО ВЫБОРА**
H1: **Не больше управленческих решений. Точнее выбор.**
Support:
When several solutions look reasonable, проSTRAнство helps establish which has grounds in this situation and tests the choice in action.
Primary CTA: **Разобрать управленческую ситуацию**
Secondary CTA: **Как работает технология**
Visual: canonical owner-locked Hero image only.
No dashboard overlay, no generated mobile substitute.

### HOME-02 — THE REAL DECISION PROBLEM
Question: **Почему разумного решения недостаточно?**
Purpose: change the visitor’s frame from “find an idea” to “establish grounds for choice”.
Content logic:
- several explanations can fit the same symptom;
- functions can hold different versions of reality;
- familiar frameworks can all appear applicable;
- changing what already works can be as expensive as failing to change what is broken.
Closing proposition:
**Правдоподобность — ещё не основание для действия.**

### HOME-03 — DECISION SPACE / NARROWING LOGIC
Question: **Как сужается пространство выбора?**
Show the technology mechanism without catalog overload:
1 symptom ≠ diagnosis;
2 Problem Unit / verified problem;
3 current contour: works / broken / do-not-touch;
4 confirmed triggers;
5 bounded strategic working set;
6 Bridge;
7 limited operational set;
8 test in Sprint.
Core message:
**проSTRAнство не расширяет список инициатив. Оно последовательно сокращает число решений, которые имеют право перейти в действие.**

### HOME-04 — EVIDENCE DISCIPLINE
Question: **На каком основании вариант остаётся в работе?**
Explain evidence chain:
checked conclusion → fact → source → artifact → verification owner → impact/verdict → stop-line.
Show that report/process/metric existing is not equal to working.
Feature the four current diagnostic verdicts:
Go / Conditional Go / Hold / Stop.
Important message:
**Hold и Stop — не провал процесса. Это допустимый результат точного выбора.**

### HOME-05 — WHAT THE TECHNOLOGY CAN PROTECT
Question: **Что проSTRAнство помогает не сделать?**
This is a differentiating section, not fear marketing.
Examples:
- не лечить симптом как причину;
- не менять то, что уже работает;
- не запускать стратегическую модель без подтверждённого триггера;
- не переводить в Sprint решение без Bridge;
- не запускать слишком много operational instruments simultaneously;
- не продолжать when facts/mandate/owner/object are absent.
Message:
**Качество выбора видно не только по тому, что запущено, но и по тому, что обоснованно не запущено.**

### HOME-06 — FROM CHOICE TO REALITY
Question: **Как выбранное решение встречается с реальностью?**
Explain Bridge and Sprint as verification, not generic implementation.
Sequence:
strategic output → operational carrier → owner → metric → rhythm → baseline → 30/60/90 observation → verdict.
Message:
**Исполнение здесь не финал стратегии. Это проверка качества выбора.**

### HOME-07 — PRODUCT CONTOUR
Question: **Как устроена работа с компанией?**
Four products, not automatic funnel:
1. Диагностика — mandatory paid entry; establishes problem/contour/evidence/readiness.
2. Сессия — only after supported transition; narrows choice and builds Bridge/Sprint charter.
3. Спринт 30–60–90 — tests selected contour with owners, baseline, metrics, decision log.
4. Сопровождение — only if active post-Sprint object exists; checks sustainability.
Explicit return/stop paths must be visible.
Do not imply purchase of all four.

### HOME-08 — WHAT THE CLIENT RECEIVES
Artifact-led proof rather than vague benefits.
Grouped by decision stage:
- Problem Unit map / current-contour map / trigger register / diagnostic verdict;
- STR working set / OPE working set / Bridge map / Sprint charter;
- baseline / decision log / Sprint verdict;
- active-contour passport / drift map / Support verdict.
Purpose: make the technology tangible.

### HOME-09 — DEPTH WITHOUT CATALOG
Question: **Откуда берётся выбор, если на экране нет каталога?**
Use 95 accepted unique STR and 115 accepted unique instrument core only as evidence of expert depth.
Do NOT present as “95/115 solutions”.
Message:
**Глубина библиотеки нужна системе, чтобы клиенту не пришлось выбирать из библиотеки.**
Explain bounded selection: 5–7 strategic frameworks as working set; ultimately 3–5 operational instruments in Sprint.

### HOME-10 — CASES
Purpose: demonstrate that different management situations produce different routes and verdicts.
Use generalized cases only; clearly label them as generalized management examples, not named-company outcomes.
Case cards should show:
situation → what initially looked plausible → what evidence changed → selected/rejected direction → resulting verdict/next step.
Include Hold/Stop examples where corpus supports them.
No invented financial outcomes.

### HOME-11 — ANTI-CATEGORY / COMPARISON
Heading concept: **Что это — и чем проSTRAнство не является**
Compare:
- not ordinary strategy session;
- not audit/report;
- not framework catalog;
- not outsourced project office;
- not training;
- not AI product.
Positive counter-definition: system establishing what is justified to do — and what is not — in this situation.

### HOME-12 — ENTRY / QUALIFICATION
Heading: **Начать не с решения. Начать с ситуации.**
Explain qualification before paid Diagnostics:
management problem/pain;
mandate;
access to facts/data;
readiness to accept adverse verdict.
Primary CTA: **Разобрать управленческую ситуацию**
No free-audit framing.

### HOME-13 — FAQ
Questions should resolve purchase/category objections, not repeat marketing:
- Why not start with a strategic session?
- Can Diagnostics end with Hold/Stop?
- Is Session automatic?
- Why only 3–5 instruments in Sprint?
- What if part of the contour already works?
- Is this consulting / project office?
- What evidence is required?
- What happens after 90 days?
FAQ content source-locked at S08.

### HOME-14 — FINAL CTA
Return to governing idea:
**Не больше решений. Больше оснований для выбора.**
CTA: **Разобрать управленческую ситуацию**
Support: first step is qualification for paid Diagnostics, not commitment to the full product contour.

## 4. Supporting page architecture

### /how-it-works/
Deep explanation of Problem Unit → triggers → selection → Bridge → Sprint → verdicts.
Purpose: mechanism proof.

### /diagnostics/
Paid entry product. Qualification, inputs, six criteria, four verdicts, artifacts, boundaries, next-step logic.

### /session/
Confirmed diagnosis → bounded STR set → operational candidates → Bridge → Sprint charter. Explicitly not a generic strategy session.

### /sprint/
3–5 operational instruments; owners; baseline; rhythm; decision log; 30/60/90; possible scale/continue/rebuild/stop/transfer.

### /support/
Only active post-Sprint contour; sustainability/drift; continue/reduce/new Sprint/rebuild/exit.

### /cases/
Generalized cases with evidence-safe template. No named-client implication.

### /technology/
Definition, evidence discipline, source/depth explanation, anti-category, roles/boundaries. 95/115 only in context.

### /faq/
Master FAQ grouped by: category, process, verdicts, products, evidence, commercial entry.

## 5. UX rules
- One dominant question per section.
- Each section must advance the decision story; no repeated “benefit cards”.
- Explain mechanism before product sale.
- Evidence before claims.
- Products are conditional transitions, not a linear package ladder.
- “Do not touch”, Hold and Stop remain visible.
- Internal codes and engineering vocabulary stay below first layer.
- Russian-first terminology.
- No framework/tool catalog UI.
- No SaaS/dashboard metaphor.
- No AI-first framing.
- Hero image remains owner-locked and is not regenerated.

## 6. Page-story sequence
Visitor cognition:
1 “I need a solution.”
2 “Several plausible solutions may fit the same symptom.”
3 “The real task is to establish grounds for choice.”
4 “I see how the technology narrows the field.”
5 “I see that it can reject action, not only recommend it.”
6 “I see how a selected choice is translated and tested.”
7 “I understand what I receive as artifacts.”
8 “I see depth without being forced into a catalog.”
9 “I see evidence-safe examples.”
10 “I understand what this is not.”
11 “I can enter through a bounded paid Diagnostics without buying a transformation.”

## 7. Conversion architecture
Primary conversion: qualification request for paid Diagnostics.
Secondary conversion: mechanism exploration.
No fake lead magnet, free audit, instant score, calculator or “choose your framework” quiz unless separately approved later.
