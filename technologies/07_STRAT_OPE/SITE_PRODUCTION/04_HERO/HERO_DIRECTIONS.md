# S04 — EARLY HERO DIRECTIONS — проSTRAнство
Status: OWNER SELECTION REQUIRED BEFORE FINAL HERO
Dependency: M LOCK

## Semantic lock
Governing message: «Не больше управленческих решений. Точнее выбор.»
Positioning: экспертная система управленческого выбора.
Core visual logic: пространство возможных решений → доказательное сужение → рабочий выбор → проверка реальностью.

Existing visualization corpus confirms “сужение” as native technology logic, but existing slides are not site Hero masters and their old “problem→execution” message does not govern the Hero.

## Direction A — «Поле решений»
Visual thesis:
A broad structured field of many plausible routes occupies the left/center. They are not chaotic scribbles: each looks legitimate. Evidence filters progressively remove routes. One route survives and becomes a precise executable line entering a small verification contour.

Meaning:
The danger is not chaos itself; it is that several alternatives look reasonable.
проSTRAнство does not invent options — it reduces them.

Strength:
Most direct visualization of “пространство управленческого выбора”.
Strong connection to brand name without literal typography inside image.
Can become a distinctive site visual system.

Risk:
Must avoid looking like network routing, metro map or software architecture.

## Direction B — «Линза выбора»
Visual thesis:
A wide field of competing management signals/routes passes through a rigorous analytical aperture/lens. On the other side, one narrow coherent route emerges and enters a verification frame.

Meaning:
The technology changes resolution: from many plausible interpretations to a justified choice.

Strength:
Immediately understandable; premium and minimal.
Can communicate precision without showing internal mechanics.

Risk:
Lens metaphor can drift into optics/camera/AI if rendered literally. Must remain abstract and managerial.

## Direction C — «Карта пространства»
Visual thesis:
An executive decision map seen obliquely: multiple territories/routes, boundaries, constraints and intersections. A controlled narrowing marks the admissible corridor; one route is highlighted not as “winner” but as the route supported by current evidence. At the far edge: a verification checkpoint.

Meaning:
Choice is contextual: the right route exists inside constraints of this specific company/situation.

Strength:
Deepest semantic use of “проSTRAнство”; visually ownable; avoids generic funnel.
Allows later site sections to zoom into areas of the same “decision space”.

Risk:
More complex to generate cleanly. Must avoid geographic/topographic/game-map appearance.

## Direction D — «Сечение»
Visual thesis:
A dense stack of translucent decision planes/options is progressively cut by several evidence planes. At their intersection remains a narrow solid corridor/object; it continues into an operational test zone.

Meaning:
A decision is not selected by taste. It survives several independent constraints.

Strength:
Feels analytical, engineered and premium.
Visually expresses evidence without literal checklists.

Risk:
Can become decorative 3D glass if aesthetics dominate meaning.

## Direction E — «Отсечение»
Visual thesis:
Many equal, credible branches begin from one management situation. At successive evidence gates, most terminate cleanly. A small number survive; finally one bounded route proceeds to execution test. Rejected routes remain visible as deliberate “not now / not this”.

Meaning:
The technology creates value not only by choosing what to do, but by establishing what not to do.

Strength:
Makes Hold/Stop and “не трогать” part of the positioning.
Sharp contrast with consulting that adds initiatives.

Risk:
If too aggressive, may read as loss/failure instead of precision.
